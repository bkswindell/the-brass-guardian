## Complete Plate Description — Visual Evidence Only

The artifact is a damaged Academy catalog sheet with the most important evidence physically removed. The page is creased, torn, stained, and split at the edges. An Academy or archive emblem appears at upper left.

The center contains an empty portrait-oriented photograph mount. Four metal or dark-card mounting corners remain, but the original image has been taken away. The blank paper inside the mount is discolored, indicating that a photograph once covered it.

Several layers of institutional interference are visible:

- the accession number is crossed out;
- the technical-removal statement is struck through with black bars;
- the handling instructions are completely redacted;
- a large red missing-status box states that the item was removed from the collection and official records;
- a High Council notice declares the record altered and further inquiry treason;
- a red `X` marks Prototype I in the Academy index;
- the bottom sealing notice cites Directive VII-19.

A torn handwritten inventor-log fragment is taped over the left side. The High Council seal overlaps the alteration notice. A faint circular Academy seal is visible behind the left-side description and another faint seal sits behind the redacted handling instructions.

The plate is designed as evidence of absence: missing photograph, erased accession, sealed references, redactions, and conflicting institutional traces.

## Non-Visual Canon References and Story Context

This plate is the primary visual evidence that Prototype I existed and that its record was deliberately altered. The hidden sequence governing its confiscation, [Underclock](../organizations/The_Underclock.md) extraction, [Ninth Guild](../organizations/The_Ninth_Guild.md) manipulation, and unresolved location belongs to [The Disappearance of Prototype I](../story_arcs/The_Disappearance_of_Prototype_I.md) and [The Black Catalogue Arc](../story_arcs/The_Black_Catalogue_Arc.md).

The plate describes Prototype I as the earliest full-scale [Heart Engine](../locations/The_Aetherium.md) iteration. That is an in-world record and may itself be incomplete or institutionally distorted.

The four redacted handling instructions are visually present but unreadable. Their content must not be invented unless later canon explicitly restores it.
