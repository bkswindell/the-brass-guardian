## Complete Plate Description — Visual Evidence Only

The plate combines a large sepia photograph of the completed right-arm gauntlet with a detailed lateral technical drawing and several component studies.

The gauntlet is a full articulated mechanical arm extending from an enclosed forearm cuff to individually jointed fingers. Brass plates, steel ribs, narrow pipes, small piston rods, cable-like tendons, circular bearings, and dense gearing are layered over a darker structural frame. The Aether Heart appears as a bright blue-white circular core mounted prominently near the wrist/forearm junction beneath concentric protective rings.

The central technical elevation exposes the structure more clearly. Multiple leader lines identify pressure regulation, conduit distribution, tendon assemblies, interlock gearing, heat-damping mechanisms, the palm anchor, and articulated digits. The outer cuff contains larger cylinders, manifolds, and a reinforced mounting structure.

Three lower details show:

1. a close photograph of the glowing Aether Heart housing;
2. a measured lateral line drawing of the entire right arm;
3. a close photograph of the elbow-joint assembly.

A square sample of dark unknown alloy is pinned at lower left. The surface is scratched and riveted, with a warmer metallic corner exposed. Elias’s handwritten modifications and signature occupy a separate taped note at lower right, accompanied by a circular construction diagram and a small gear emblem.

The sheet is worn and repaired with tape. No redactions or institutional seals are visible beyond the gear emblem and archive control fields.

## Non-Visual Canon References and Story Context

This plate is authoritative for the external visual construction of [Amelia Hawthorne](../characters/Amelia_Hawthorne.md)’s Aether Gauntlet and the persistent visible placement of the Aether Heart.

The image’s statement that [Elias](../characters/Professor_Elias_Hawthorne.md) designed and constructed the arm is an archival claim. Later canon concerning Prototype I, Prototype II, unknown components, and institutional intervention may complicate the completeness of that statement. See [The Black Catalogue Arc](../story_arcs/The_Black_Catalogue_Arc.md), [Prototype II Cabinet Photograph](011_Prototype_II_Cabinet_Photograph.md), and [The Missing Prototype I Catalog Card](012_The_Missing_Prototype_I_Catalog_Card.md).

The plate should not be used to treat [Amelia](../characters/Amelia_Hawthorne.md) as equipment. It documents the prosthetic mechanism, not ownership of the person wearing it.
