## Complete Plate Description — Visual Evidence Only

The active image is a wide, cinematic interior view rather than an archival architectural lithograph.

The viewer looks through an open pair of monumental black-and-brass gates into a vast glass-domed atrium. The gates are filled with gears, circular mechanisms, scrollwork, lamps, and botanical ornament. The right gate carries the oval **THE GRAND ATRIUM** sign.

The interior contains:

- a high hemispherical glass roof supported by intricate brass ribs;
- suspended rings, gears, and orrery-like mechanisms beneath the dome;
- a large central armillary or orrery mounted on a pedestal;
- a circular flower bed surrounding the central mechanism;
- multiple clocks, including a prominent clock tower at left;
- tiered galleries and balconies;
- ornate staircases rising at the rear and right;
- warm gas or aether lamps throughout;
- dense greenery, flowers, planters, and climbing vegetation;
- polished dark stone flooring inlaid with brass circles and geometric diagrams;
- visible blue sky and distant Aetherhaven architecture beyond the glass.

The visual design is symmetrical but not rigid. Mechanical structures and living plants are interwoven throughout. Warm amber light reflects from brass and wet-looking polished stone, while cool blue daylight enters through the dome.

No hidden maintenance passages or sealed omitted doorway can be verified from the active image. Those features belong to the intended artifact concept, not the visible evidence.

## Non-Visual Canon References and Story Context

The artifact slate originally calls for an antique elevation and cutaway showing the central orrery, hidden maintenance passages, and a sealed doorway omitted from public plans. The active art currently serves as a canonical location view of the Grand Atrium, not as that planned technical plate.
