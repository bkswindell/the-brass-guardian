# The Mechanists' Guild

## Canonical Summary

The Mechanists' Guild is [Aetherhaven](../locations/Aetherhaven.md)'s professional order governing engineers, repair workers, automata builders, engine operators, structural specialists, canal engineers, aether technicians, clockmakers, artificers, and other certified practitioners responsible for the city's machinery and infrastructure.

It is not one of [the Eight Founding Engineering Guilds](The_Eight_Founding_Engineering_Guilds.md).

It is the modern federation that unites them.

The Guild sets common safety standards, certifies professional rank, investigates major mechanical failures, mediates disputes among disciplines, and approves nearly every major machine connected to [Aetherhaven](../locations/Aetherhaven.md)'s public systems.

Its members carry brass medallions engraved with:

- their founding guild,
- certified discipline,
- rank,
- and right to practice.

Some older medallions contain a second symbol hidden beneath the official seal.

The symbol matches architectural patterns found inside [the Heart Engine](../locations/The_Aetherium.md).

No current guild record explains why.

## Public Role

The Mechanists' Guild exists to ensure that the machines supporting [Aetherhaven](../locations/Aetherhaven.md) are built, maintained, and operated by people who understand their responsibilities.

Its authority covers:

- professional certification,
- apprenticeships,
- safety standards,
- public-machine inspection,
- accident review,
- repair authorization,
- hazardous-system access,
- and disciplinary action against negligent practitioners.

A [High Council](The_High_Council_of_Aetherhaven.md) permit may authorize a project.

A Mechanists' Guild seal certifies that someone is willing to be professionally responsible for it.

## The Eight Founding Orders

The Guild is composed of [the Eight Founding Engineering Guilds](The_Eight_Founding_Engineering_Guilds.md):

1. [The Guild of Framewrights](The_Eight_Founding_Engineering_Guilds.md)
2. [The Guild of Enginewrights](The_Eight_Founding_Engineering_Guilds.md)
3. [The Guild of Aetherwrights](The_Eight_Founding_Engineering_Guilds.md)
4. [The Guild of Canalwrights](The_Eight_Founding_Engineering_Guilds.md)
5. [The Guild of Skywrights](The_Eight_Founding_Engineering_Guilds.md)
6. [The Guild of Clockwrights](The_Eight_Founding_Engineering_Guilds.md)
7. [The Guild of Artificers](The_Eight_Founding_Engineering_Guilds.md)
8. [The Guild of Verdant Mechanists](The_Eight_Founding_Engineering_Guilds.md)

Each retains its own traditions, symbol, archives, and technical authority.

Their guildmasters form the **[Conclave of Eight](The_Conclave_of_Eight.md)**.

For full founding-guild canon, see [The Eight Founding Engineering Guilds](The_Eight_Founding_Engineering_Guilds.md).

## [The Octagonal Hall](../locations/The_Octagonal_Hall.md)

The Mechanists' Guild is headquartered in the **[Octagonal Hall](../locations/The_Octagonal_Hall.md)**, positioned between [the Inventors' District](../locations/The_Inventors_District.md) and [Government District](../locations/The_Government_District.md).

Each of its eight sides belongs symbolically and administratively to one founding guild.

The Hall contains:

- Conclave chambers,
- certification courts,
- disciplinary rooms,
- technical archives,
- apprenticeship registries,
- accident records,
- public standards offices,
- and sealed collections of obsolete guild law.

At the center stands a circular representation of [the Aetherium](../locations/The_Aetherium.md).

Visitors are told it honors the source of [Aetherhaven](../locations/Aetherhaven.md)'s power.

Older members sometimes avoid standing directly upon it.

## [The Conclave of Eight](The_Conclave_of_Eight.md)

[The Conclave](The_Conclave_of_Eight.md) consists of the eight founding guildmasters.

It decides:

- citywide safety standards,
- inter-guild disputes,
- emergency engineering policy,
- professional sanctions,
- access to restricted infrastructure,
- and whether new classes of machinery may be certified.

[The Conclave](The_Conclave_of_Eight.md) advises [the High Council](The_High_Council_of_Aetherhaven.md) but is not formally subordinate to it in technical judgment.

This independence creates recurring political conflict.

A Council official may demand that a project proceed.

[The Conclave](The_Conclave_of_Eight.md) may refuse to certify it.

The city then decides whether authority or safety matters more.

## Professional Ranks

Mechanists' Guild ranks may vary in title between disciplines, but the federation recognizes a common structure.

### Apprentice

A student formally bound to a certified workshop, master, or guild school.

### Certified Hand

A practitioner approved for supervised work in a recognized specialty.

### Journeyman

A fully mobile professional permitted to work independently within certified limits.

### Master Mechanist

A senior practitioner authorized to certify work, supervise apprentices, and accept professional responsibility for major systems.

### Guildmaster

The elected or recognized head of one founding guild and a member of [the Conclave of Eight](The_Conclave_of_Eight.md).

Special offices may exist beyond rank, including:

- Inspector,
- Examiner,
- Keeper,
- Warden,
- and Chief Engineer.

## Guild Medallions

Every certified member carries a brass medallion.

A medallion identifies:

- rank,
- discipline,
- guild affiliation,
- certification region,
- and authorization level.

Modern medallions are standardized and difficult to counterfeit.

Older medallions are more varied.

Some contain a second engraved pattern concealed beneath:

- a removable seal,
- layered brass,
- oxidation,
- or an official rank plate.

The hidden pattern resembles:

- conduit geometry,
- access markings,
- and structural motifs found inside [the Heart Engine](../locations/The_Aetherium.md).

Possible explanations include:

- the first guilds copied ancient architecture without understanding it,
- early medallions served as access tokens,
- the Guild inherited symbols from an older order,
- or the public seal was designed to conceal the true mark.

None has been confirmed.

## Safety Standards

The Guild's standards govern:

- pressure tolerances,
- structural load,
- aether shielding,
- emergency shutdowns,
- public access,
- automaton command limits,
- maintenance intervals,
- and operator responsibility.

Its most important principle is:

> A machine must fail more safely than the people around it.

Not every ancient mechanism obeys that principle.

This is one reason the Guild distrusts uncontrolled excavation and unauthorized activation.

## Investigation of Failures

When a major machine fails, the Guild may conduct its own technical inquiry alongside [the Brass Watch](The_Brass_Watch.md).

The Watch asks:

- Was a crime committed?
- Who is responsible?
- Is the public in danger?

The Guild asks:

- What failed?
- Why did it fail?
- Was the design, maintenance, material, or operator responsible?
- Can the failure happen again?

These investigations may cooperate or conflict.

A Guild inquiry can expose negligence.

It can also become a way to protect powerful members from criminal accountability.

## Relationship with [the High Council](The_High_Council_of_Aetherhaven.md)

[The High Council](The_High_Council_of_Aetherhaven.md) depends on the Guild for technical legitimacy.

The Guild depends on the Council for legal enforcement, public contracts, and access to civic infrastructure.

Neither fully trusts the other.

Council officials may pressure the Guild to:

- approve politically important projects,
- conceal failures,
- accelerate certification,
- or deny inconvenient findings.

[The Conclave](The_Conclave_of_Eight.md) may respond through:

- refusal to certify,
- professional censure,
- work stoppage,
- or public technical findings.

## Relationship with [the Academy of Invention](The_Academy_of_Invention.md)

[The Academy](The_Academy_of_Invention.md) teaches theory and develops experimental designs.

The Guild decides whether those designs are safe enough for public practice.

This creates a longstanding rivalry.

Academy scholars often consider the Guild cautious and old-fashioned.

Guild masters often consider [the Academy](The_Academy_of_Invention.md) brilliant and insufficiently accountable.

Many [Ninth Guild](The_Ninth_Guild.md) researchers began as people caught between those two cultures.

## Relationship with [the Brass Watch](The_Brass_Watch.md)

The Guild certifies and repairs many [Brass Watch](The_Brass_Watch.md) systems, including:

- clockwork sentinels,
- forensic instruments,
- emergency mechanisms,
- pressure barriers,
- and evidence-storage devices.

The Watch depends on Guild expertise during industrial accidents and anomalous-machine investigations.

[Chief Inspector Thorne](../characters/Chief_Inspector_Beatrice_Thorne.md) distrusts any inquiry in which the Guild investigates its own members without independent oversight.

The Guild distrusts Watch officers who seize machinery before its condition can be properly documented.

## Relationship with [the Aerial Mariners' Union](The_Aerial_Mariners_Union.md)

The Guild's [Skywrights](The_Guild_of_Skywrights.md) and [Enginewrights](The_Guild_of_Enginewrights.md) certify airships and major port machinery.

[The Aerial Mariners' Union](The_Aerial_Mariners_Union.md) operates those systems in real conditions.

This produces an enduring disagreement:

- the Guild trusts design tolerances,
- the Union trusts lived experience,
- and [Captain Mara Voss](../characters/Captain_Mara_Voss.md) listens to whichever one believes the storm is about to prove right.

The Union can refuse to operate a certified vessel.

The Guild can refuse to certify one the Union wants to fly.

## Relationship with [the Ninth Guild](The_Ninth_Guild.md)

[The Ninth Guild](The_Ninth_Guild.md) represents the Mechanists' Guild's most troubling shadow.

Some [Ninth Guild](The_Ninth_Guild.md) members are:

- expelled masters,
- censured researchers,
- failed apprentices,
- or certified practitioners who continued prohibited work in secret.

The Mechanists' Guild officially condemns:

- memory grafting,
- conscious automata without legal review,
- emotional-energy engines,
- identity revision,
- and unsafe synthesis of living and mechanical systems.

[The Ninth Guild](The_Ninth_Guild.md) argues that the official Guild's safety rules have become tools for suppressing discovery.

[The Black Lacuna](The_Ninth_Guild.md) deliberately mocks the recognized eight-guild structure.

## Relationship with [Elias Hawthorne](../characters/Professor_Elias_Hawthorne.md)

[Elias](../characters/Professor_Elias_Hawthorne.md) is respected for his practical engineering and criticized for operating beyond ordinary certification boundaries.

His work on:

- the Wayfinder,
- ancient mechanisms,
- and [Amelia](../characters/Amelia_Hawthorne.md)'s Aether Gauntlet

creates intense professional interest.

Some Guild members regard him as a master innovator.

Others believe his discoveries are too dependent on systems he cannot fully explain.

[Elias](../characters/Professor_Elias_Hawthorne.md) respects good standards.

He distrusts any standard used to forbid investigation merely because the answer may be politically uncomfortable.

## Relationship with [Amelia Hawthorne](../characters/Amelia_Hawthorne.md)

The Guild's relationship with [Amelia](../characters/Amelia_Hawthorne.md) is ethically delicate.

Her gauntlet crosses multiple disciplines:

- Artificer,
- Aetherwright,
- Enginewright,
- and possibly Verdant Mechanist.

No current certification system adequately describes it.

Some members want to study and classify the gauntlet.

Others believe it falls outside professional authority because it is part of [Amelia](../characters/Amelia_Hawthorne.md)'s body and identity.

The Guild must never treat [Amelia](../characters/Amelia_Hawthorne.md) as a machine requiring certification.

A strong internal principle should emerge:

> The Guild may certify the work performed upon a person. It does not certify the person.

## Temporal Significance

The [Clockwrights](The_Guild_of_Clockwrights.md)' archives, old medallions, and hidden symbols connect the Guild to [Aetherhaven](../locations/Aetherhaven.md)'s temporal mysteries.

Possible evidence includes:

- medallions dated before the Guild's official founding,
- rank seals matching [Heart Engine](../locations/The_Aetherium.md) access patterns,
- guild charters with changing signatures,
- and certification records for machines that do not yet exist.

The Guild has historically treated these as archival errors.

That explanation is becoming harder to maintain.

## Medical Devices, Prosthetics, and Implants

The Guild works with [the Order of the Mended Hand](The_Order_of_the_Mended_Hand.md) wherever a certified machine becomes part of medical treatment, rehabilitation, mobility, sensory support, or a person's embodied life.

The Guild governs engineering standards and professional mechanical responsibility. The Order governs medical care, consent, and biological safety.

Neither institution may claim ownership of a patient or assert that certification grants authority over a device after it becomes integrated with that person's body.

Ancient components such as Amelia's Aether Heart create an unresolved conflict because they cannot be certified under known standards and cannot be separated from the patient merely to satisfy professional review.

## Relationship with the Order of the Mended Hand

The Guild collaborates with [the Order of the Mended Hand](The_Order_of_the_Mended_Hand.md) on prosthetics, braces, implants, and adaptive machinery.

This collaboration saves lives but can favor replacement over biological recovery. A damaged limb may be treated as an engineering problem before every medical or herbal option has been exhausted.

Guild certification does not guarantee that an intervention is the best choice for the patient.

## Continuity Constraints

- The Mechanists' Guild is the modern federation of [the Eight Founding Engineering Guilds](The_Eight_Founding_Engineering_Guilds.md).
- It is not itself a ninth founding guild.
- It must remain both a safety institution and a political power.
- Most members are honorable professionals, though corruption and institutional self-protection can exist.
- Member medallions identify rank and discipline.
- Hidden symbols beneath old medallions must match [Heart Engine](../locations/The_Aetherium.md) architecture.
- The meaning of the hidden symbols remains unresolved early.
- The Guild must not claim ownership or certification authority over [Amelia](../characters/Amelia_Hawthorne.md) as a person.
- [The Conclave of Eight](The_Conclave_of_Eight.md) meets in [the Octagonal Hall](../locations/The_Octagonal_Hall.md).
- [The Ninth Guild](The_Ninth_Guild.md) remains a forbidden shadow, not an acknowledged member.
- The Guild should generate meaningful ethical and professional conflicts rather than simple bureaucracy.
