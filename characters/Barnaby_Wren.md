# Barnaby Wren

> **Placeholder profile.** This file preserves all currently supplied descriptions of Barnaby Wren without inventing an explanation for their temporal implications.

## Canonical Summary

The newest compiled source identifies Barnaby as the proprietor of [the Last Lantern](../locations/The_Last_Lantern.md), the oldest shop in [the Merchant District](../locations/The_Merchant_District.md). His shelves contain impossible objects including unopened letters from the future, keys without locks, maps of places that may not exist, and bottles filled with forgotten songs. He accepts no money; his price is always a promise. He is warm toward [Amelia](Amelia_Hawthorne.md) and distrustful of [Elias](Professor_Elias_Hawthorne.md), whom he accuses of already breaking a promise [Elias](Professor_Elias_Hawthorne.md) does not remember making.

Earlier supplied material also describes Barnaby as a retired explorer and storyteller whose shop walls are covered in impossible maps. Records claim that he once vanished for two years, although he remembers only a few weeks.

Both descriptions are retained pending a fuller character profile.
