## Canonical Summary

The newest compiled source presents Gideon Brasswell as the broad-shouldered, soot-covered chief engineer responsible for delivering power from [the Heart Engine](../locations/The_Aetherium.md) through [Aetherhaven](../locations/Aetherhaven.md). He trusts pressure gauges more than politicians, initially dismisses stories of ancient intelligence, has heard breathing behind the walls of [the Pulse Chamber](../locations/The_Pulse_Chamber.md), and believes something beneath the city is awakening.

Earlier supplied material also describes Gideon as a respected master engineer who maintains ancient machinery, has two mechanical hands, trained [Elias Hawthorne](Professor_Elias_Hawthorne.md) as an apprentice, and carries an old guild medallion whose markings resemble patterns inside [the Heart Engine](../locations/The_Aetherium.md).

Both descriptions are retained here pending reconciliation.
