# Tamsin Pike

## Canonical Summary

Tamsin Pike is a clever, practical young canal guide with an unusual knowledge of locks, maintenance passages, water levels, and doors that do not appear on ordinary maps.

She carries an old brass key whose design predates several of [Aetherhaven](../locations/Aetherhaven.md)'s current canal systems.

Tamsin may already belong to [the Underclock](../organizations/The_Underclock.md).

Or [the Underclock](../organizations/The_Underclock.md) may believe she belongs to it because of a promise, route, debt, or protection inherited from the Pike family.

She is comfortable around smugglers and guides without romanticizing them. She understands that hidden routes can rescue people, enrich criminals, conceal abuse, and preserve truths official institutions would rather erase.

Tamsin is loyal once trust has been earned.

She dislikes being treated as a child who wandered into secrets she does not understand.

The truth is more complicated:

She understands many of the routes.

She does not yet understand why some of them recognize her key.

## Public Identity

Tamsin is known as a capable canal child or young route guide who can:

- identify old locks,
- read water movement,
- enter maintenance corridors,
- repair simple mechanisms,
- and remember complicated paths.

Adults may underestimate her because her knowledge is practical rather than academic.

## The Brass Key

Tamsin's key may represent:

- access to an old canal route,
- an inherited [Underclock](../organizations/The_Underclock.md) obligation,
- proof of sanctuary,
- a family Compact,
- or a mechanism involved in the Prototype I extraction.

The key should not open every door.

Its importance comes from the specific histories and promises attached to it.

## Relationship with [the Underclock](../organizations/The_Underclock.md)

Some [Underclock](../organizations/The_Underclock.md) members recognize Tamsin immediately.

Others know only the key.

Possible forms of her status include:

- occasional Canal Runner,
- route heir,
- protected family member,
- messenger,
- or holder of an unfinished obligation.

Tamsin may have performed small jobs without ever taking a formal oath.

Her larger arc involves choosing what, if anything, she owes to a network whose secrecy has protected people and hidden serious mistakes.

For complete organization canon, see [The Underclock](../organizations/The_Underclock.md).

## Relationship with [Amelia Hawthorne](Amelia_Hawthorne.md)

Tamsin can become a close friend and grounded peer for [Amelia](Amelia_Hawthorne.md).

She offers:

- local knowledge,
- practical skepticism,
- humor,
- and willingness to enter places adults have declared inaccessible.

[Amelia](Amelia_Hawthorne.md) sees patterns Tamsin does not.

Tamsin knows routes [Amelia](Amelia_Hawthorne.md) cannot read.

Their friendship should be mutual rather than reducing Tamsin to a guide.

## Relationship with [Elias Hawthorne](Professor_Elias_Hawthorne.md)

[Elias](Professor_Elias_Hawthorne.md) respects Tamsin's competence but worries about:

- hidden obligations,
- dangerous routes,
- and adults using her knowledge.

Tamsin resents overprotection when it becomes dismissal.

She may still trust [Elias](Professor_Elias_Hawthorne.md) because he repairs things without asking her to surrender the key.

## Visual Continuity

Tamsin should appear practical and mobile:

- weather-resistant canal clothes,
- sturdy boots,
- compact tools,
- rope or belt loops,
- a small route lantern,
- and the distinctive brass key.

Her clothing may include inherited repairs or old canal-family markings.

## Staged Revelations

### Early

- Tamsin knows hidden canal routes.
- She carries an unusual brass key.
- [Underclock](../organizations/The_Underclock.md) people recognize her or the key.
- She avoids giving a direct answer about membership.

### Middle

- The Pike family held a route obligation.
- Her key is connected to an old Compact.
- The key may have been used during the Prototype I extraction.
- Tamsin must decide whether to protect the network or expose a harmful secret.

### Later

- What the key truly opens.
- Which Pike relative carried it before her.
- Whether Tamsin was granted sanctuary as a child.
- Why an ancient route responds to her presence.

## Continuity Constraints

- Tamsin must remain more than a route guide.
- Her [Underclock](../organizations/The_Underclock.md) connection should be substantial but initially ambiguous.
- She must not be treated as automatically criminal.
- Her practical intelligence should remain distinct from [Amelia](Amelia_Hawthorne.md)'s pattern recognition and [Elias](Professor_Elias_Hawthorne.md)'s engineering knowledge.
- The brass key must have limited, specific significance rather than functioning as a universal key.
- Tamsin's choices must determine her eventual place in [the Underclock](../organizations/The_Underclock.md).
