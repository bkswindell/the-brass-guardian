# The Clockwork Jungle Expedition

> **Canonical historical event.** This file owns the limited objective history currently established for the expedition and disaster. [Return to the Clockwork Jungle](../story_arcs/The_Return_to_the_Clockwork_Jungle.md) owns the later story in which Amelia, Elias, and the reader revisit the site and test the surviving accounts.

## Canonical Historical Summary

Before the opening of the main series, [Professor Elias Hawthorne](../characters/Professor_Elias_Hawthorne.md) and [Amelia Hawthorne](../characters/Amelia_Hawthorne.md) participated in an expedition into [the Clockwork Jungle](../locations/The_Clockwork_Jungle.md), a region where living wilderness, mechanical growth, and Ancient structures exist together in ways not yet fully understood.

The expedition was connected to Elias's study of Ancient technology.

Within a hidden structure, chamber, or vault, a dormant Ancient machine awakened.

An explosion followed.

Much of the expedition was destroyed. Amelia's right arm was catastrophically injured, and she narrowly survived. Elias survived and later constructed the mechanical framework that became Amelia's [Aether Gauntlet](../artifacts/009_The_Aether_Gauntlet_Exterior_Study.md).

The gauntlet now contains the visible [Aether Heart](../artifacts/009_The_Aether_Gauntlet_Exterior_Study.md) and functions as an interface with Ancient machinery. Whether the Heart was recovered during the expedition, introduced during Amelia's treatment, awakened later, or connected to her before the explosion remains unresolved.

Elias spent years blaming himself and studying Ancient engineering afterward.

His guilt is canonical.

His final responsibility is not yet proven.

## Public Record

The public version of the event is incomplete and may be reduced to a failed expedition followed by the remarkable survival of Elias and Amelia Hawthorne.

Citizens who know Amelia may understand that her mechanical arm resulted from an expedition accident without knowing:

- what awakened;
- why the machine responded;
- how much of the expedition was lost;
- whether other people survived;
- where the Aether Heart came from;
- or why Ancient systems continue to recognize Amelia.

The public account may emphasize Elias's engineering achievement in saving Amelia while omitting the unresolved circumstances that made the rescue necessary.

No single explanation is yet canonical.

## Institutional Responses

### Society of Explorers

The [Society of Explorers](../organizations/The_Society_of_Explorers.md) may have sponsored, approved, recorded, or later investigated the expedition. Its exact role remains unresolved.

The event may connect to the Society's changing seal, charter, and later interest in temporal or Ancient systems.

### Academy of Invention

The [Academy of Invention](../organizations/The_Academy_of_Invention.md) may possess technical records, prior research, or professional responsibility connected to Elias's work.

No formal blame or institutional involvement is yet assigned.

### Mechanists' Guild

The [Mechanists' Guild](../organizations/The_Mechanists_Guild.md) may have reviewed the construction of Amelia's arm, disputed the Ancient components, or attempted to classify the gauntlet.

No final jurisdiction is established.

### Order of the Closed Eye

The [Order of the Closed Eye](../organizations/The_Order_of_the_Closed_Eye.md) may have monitored, sealed, altered, or suppressed records connected to the site or Aether Heart.

Direct involvement remains unconfirmed.

## Order of the Mended Hand

The [Order of the Mended Hand](../organizations/The_Order_of_the_Mended_Hand.md) may have supported the expedition, recovery, surgery, rehabilitation, prosthetic integration, experimental treatment, or later monitoring.

The exact role remains unresolved.

The historical record must not imply that Elias single-handedly provided every form of care required to keep Amelia alive. His emergency engineering and stabilization may have been essential, while trained practitioners provided other critical treatment.

It must also not assume that every Hand recommendation was safe, ethical, or accepted.

The recovery may have involved disputed decisions concerning amputation, replacement, observation, the Aether Heart, or experimental integration. Elias may have accepted some measures under desperate circumstances and refused others.

Whether a Hand medic accompanied the expedition or became involved only after evacuation remains open.

## Conflicting Accounts

The following points are intentionally unresolved and may produce contradictory testimony:

- whether Elias led the expedition or served as its technical expert;
- whether Amelia was an invited participant, informal apprentice, family passenger, or unexpected presence;
- whether the machine awakened because of an action by Elias, Amelia, another expedition member, or an external event;
- whether the explosion was mechanical failure, defensive action, recognition event, temporal displacement, or attempted communication;
- whether the Aether Heart was found before, during, or after the disaster;
- whether other expedition members died, disappeared, escaped, or returned changed;
- whether Amelia remembers the event accurately, incompletely, symbolically, or through temporal interference;
- and whether Elias's private account matches the official record.

Contradictions must be preserved rather than silently reconciled.

## Continuity Constraints

- The expedition occurred before Volume 1.
- Amelia was present.
- The event was connected to Elias's Ancient-technology research.
- A dormant Ancient machine awakened in a hidden structure or vault.
- An explosion destroyed much of the expedition.
- Amelia's right arm was catastrophically injured.
- Elias later built the mechanical framework of the Aether Gauntlet.
- The Aether Heart is visibly integrated with Amelia's arm by the main series.
- Elias sincerely blames himself, but final responsibility remains unresolved.
- Elias's emergency actions were important, but Amelia's survival and recovery also required medical expertise whose personnel and sequence remain unresolved.
- The exact trigger, roster, casualties, recovery, and Heart origin remain open.
- Amelia's incomplete memory must not be dismissed as false.
- The event is revealed gradually rather than fully shown at the opening.
- A later return may revise the accepted account without invalidating the characters' emotional experiences.
- No institution may use the event to claim ownership of Amelia or the gauntlet.
