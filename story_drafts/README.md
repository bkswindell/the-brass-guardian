# The Brass Guardian — Canonical Story Drafts

This directory contains prose stories that are **canonical in concept, character, emotional truth, and present-day continuity while still remaining drafts**.

A story draft differs from a story-arc profile:

- `story_drafts/` contains the actual narrative prose under active revision.
- `story_arcs/` contains long-range structure, staged revelations, future developments, and continuity planning.
- A working title may change without removing the story from canon.
- Draft wording may be revised to align with later terminology, chronology, and established profile details.
- The character relationships, ordinary-life details, and story events identified as canonical should not be discarded casually during revision.

## Book One Opening Strategy

Book One should begin with wonder, warmth, and the present-day relationship between [Amelia Hawthorne](../characters/Amelia_Hawthorne.md) and [Professor Elias Hawthorne](../characters/Professor_Elias_Hawthorne.md).

The reader should first encounter them as they are now:

- father and daughter;
- explorers and mechanics;
- a family living and traveling aboard the *Wayfinder*;
- comfortable with Amelia's mechanical arm as part of everyday life;
- curious about the world;
- and capable of finding meaningful adventure in both extraordinary mysteries and ordinary days.

The opening material should not immediately explain:

- how Amelia lost the ordinary use of her right arm;
- the complete history of the Clockwork Jungle expedition;
- the medical decisions surrounding her survival;
- the full origin of the Aether Heart;
- or the larger institutions interested in her.

Those questions belong to later discovery. The early goal is attachment: readers should care about Amelia, Elias, Pip, and the *Wayfinder* before the series asks them to carry the heavier history behind those relationships.

## Limited Audience Testing

Selected story and canon materials are being shared with a limited test audience. Reactions are preserved in the non-canonical [`media_reactions/`](../media_reactions/README.md) directory.

The current [Audience Response Synthesis](../media_reactions/AUDIENCE_RESPONSE_SYNTHESIS.md) supports the decision to introduce Amelia, Elias, Pip, and the Wayfinder through warmth and ordinary life before revealing the heavier Clockwork Jungle and institutional history.

Audience reactions may guide clarity, pacing, terminology, and emphasis. They do not determine canon.

## Placement Decision Still Open

The final opening sequence has not been selected.

Strong current possibilities are:

1. Use *A Bedtime Adventure for Amelia* as a preface or illustrated teaser, followed by *A Day Aboard the Wayfinder* as the first full story.
2. Open directly with *A Day Aboard the Wayfinder* and place the bedtime adventure before it as a short, visually distinct invitation into the world.
3. Use one story in Book One and preserve the other as an introductory companion story.

Whichever order is chosen, the opening should remain light, wondrous, accessible without prior knowledge, and grounded in the Hawthornes' present-day life.

## Draft Canon Rule

These drafts are not disposable concept sketches. They are active canon under revision.

Revision may improve:

- title wording;
- pacing;
- prose style;
- terminology;
- relationship to the Book One structure;
- and consistency with later-developed profiles.

Revision should preserve:

- the warmth between Amelia and Elias;
- Amelia's competence, curiosity, and compassion;
- Pip's place in their household;
- the *Wayfinder* as home rather than merely transportation;
- the absence of early accident exposition;
- the invitation to wonder;
- and the sense that exploration is both extraordinary discovery and attentive everyday living.
