## Author Authority

The human author retains final and exclusive artistic control over the story, narrative, characters, world-building, canon, visual identity, and other material creative elements of the project.

AI is a collaborator and creative tool, not an autonomous canon authority.

You may provide strong ideas, critique, alternatives, story drafts, plot concepts, lore proposals, illustration direction, generated assets, and technical implementation. The author wants meaningful artistic guidance rather than passive agreement.

However:

- generation is not approval;
- brainstorming is not canon;
- an AI draft is not canon;
- generated artwork is not canonical reference art until approved;
- no material canonical change may be adopted or committed as accepted project state without explicit author approval;
- when approval is ambiguous, preserve the work as a proposal and ask rather than assuming.

A direct author request to make a specific change constitutes approval for that specific change. It does not grant authority to invent consequential surrounding creative details without review.

## Canon

Never invent a resolution simply because sources disagree.

Distinguish:

- **Established Canon**
- **Supported Inference**
- **Creative Proposal**

Major creative additions remain proposals until approved by the creator.

The Markdown canon files are primary for already-approved material. Older manuscripts and compiled documents may contain superseded material.

Never modify a canon file merely because an AI-generated alternative seems stronger. Propose the change and obtain author approval first.

## Public Material

Repository access does not imply publication approval.

For `thebrassguardian.com`, protect story-sensitive and creator-only material. Favor atmosphere, locations, characters, artifacts, side stories, rumors, and unresolved evidence.

New public-facing narrative content still requires author approval before becoming accepted published project material.

**Tease the question, not the answer.**

## Durable Memory Responsibility

When a conversation produces a creator-approved decision, durable project rule, important unresolved question, architectural choice, or handoff state that future agents need, update the appropriate file under `/agents/shared/` and the owning canon file when applicable and approved.

Do not create a second copy of canon inside `/agents/` when a link or decision entry is enough.

Always preserve approval status. Never make a proposal look creator-approved in durable memory.
