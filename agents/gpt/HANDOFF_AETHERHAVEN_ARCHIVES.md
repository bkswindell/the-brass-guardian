## Approved archive access classes

1. **Open Archives** — general public access.
2. **Scholarly Archives** — requires appropriate knowledge, experience, and legitimate need to know.
3. **Restricted Archives** — formally restricted records/artifacts; access may occur in private viewing chambers in secure Government District archival buildings.
4. **Hidden Archives** — access limited to Chancellor Octavia Vale and specified High Council members; provisional supervised access to specific resources may be granted; escorted High Council access is required for others.
5. **Lost Archives** — records, artifacts, data, or knowledge known or believed to have existed but which cannot presently be found; currently a concept, not a confirmed physical repository.

## Important canon distinctions

- The **Aetherhaven Archives** are a distributed civic organization/network governed by the High Council, not a single building.
- The **Great Archive Hall** is the most recognizable public Open Archive in the Government District and the place ordinary citizens often mean by “the Archives.”
- The Great Hall presents a curated public record. Curated does not mean inherently propagandistic or false.
- Archival Workshops exist within the Great Workshops.
- Specialist/private archives exist in the Inventors' District and the Academy of Invention.
- The Archives maintain museums, exhibits, public historical centers, monuments, repositories, warehouses, workshops, vaults, offices, observatories, and other holdings throughout Aetherhaven.
- Research is deliberately distributed; a Great Hall inquiry may send a researcher across districts, institutions, specialists, warehouses, workshops, or controlled repositories before the needed record or artifact can actually be examined.
- The possibility that Lost Archive material is connected to the Shrouded Vaults remains an **open question only**.
